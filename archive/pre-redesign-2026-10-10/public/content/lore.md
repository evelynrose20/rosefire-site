# Rosefire Lore

Rosefire's public lore lives here: information an ordinary resident could reasonably know, major organizations, notable figures, and the history that shapes current roleplay.

## Public knowledge first

This page should explain enough of the setting for a new player to create a character confidently without exposing every secret intended to be discovered through play.

## Organizations

Material for organizations such as **The Ordo** and **The Black Hand** will be added here as their public-facing lore is finalized.

## Characters

Major setting characters and figures can receive their own sections once the information that should be public is decided.

> Lore should create things to discover, not homework required before joining.
