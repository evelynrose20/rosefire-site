# Getting Started

Welcome to Rosefire. This guide will become the single starting point for new residents and founding testers.

## Before you arrive

- Read the **Rules** before joining the city.
- Join the Rosefire Discord for announcements, support, and community communication.
- Create an original character who fits naturally into the world.
- Remember that Rosefire is in active development. Founding testers should expect rough edges and report anything that blocks or damages normal play.

## Your first hours

Rosefire is being built so a new resident can arrive and begin living without waiting for a particular employee, department, or population count to come online.

Your first goals should be simple: establish your identity, open your banking access, find transportation, learn the city, secure a place to live, and discover work or a profession that interests your character.

## Testing Rosefire

Play naturally. You do not need to deliberately attack every system or follow a giant QA checklist. If something breaks during normal play, record what you were doing, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and any useful screenshot or error message.
