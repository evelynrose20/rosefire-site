# Rosefire Community & Roleplay Rules

Rosefire is built around collaborative storytelling, mutual respect, and the understanding that roleplay works best when everyone involved is trying to create a good story—not simply trying to win.

These rules establish clear expectations without trying to anticipate every possible situation. Context, intent, evidence, and impact matter.

> **“There isn't a rule saying I can't” is not permission to abuse a loophole.**

**Use reasonable judgment. Respect other players. Protect the roleplay. Do not exploit technicalities.**

## 1. Community Conduct

### Respect other players
Harassment, targeted bullying, discriminatory attacks, threats, stalking, doxxing, or repeated OOC antagonism are prohibited. IC conflict never excuses OOC hostility.

A character's opportunities are not restricted by a player's real-world identity.

### Keep IC and OOC separate
Do not carry IC arguments into Discord or OOC relationships into character interactions. Do not target a character because you dislike the player.

### Don't police other people's RP
If you believe a rule was broken, preserve useful evidence and report it. Do not interrupt scenes to argue rules, threaten reports, or punish another player yourself.

**Players roleplay. Staff handle rule enforcement.**

## 2. Roleplay Standards

### Stay in character
Remain IC during active RP unless legitimate OOC communication is necessary. Minor mistakes happen; repeatedly breaking character to argue mechanics or rules is different.

### Value your character's life
Serious danger and injury should matter. This does not require automatic obedience whenever a weapon appears. Context and reasonable escape opportunities matter.

### Powergaming
You may attempt actions; you may not unilaterally decide another character's response or use mechanics to force an outcome.

### Metagaming
Use only information your character reasonably obtained IC. Discord, streams, another character, staff tools, private channels, OOC conversations, or information heard while unconscious do not automatically become character knowledge.

Publicly documented server information may be treated as public gameplay knowledge. Secret or discovery-based information is not automatically known IC.

### Stream sniping
Do not use streams, recordings, Discord activity, or other OOC information to locate players, discover secrets, monitor situations, or gain an IC advantage.

## 3. Characters & Alternate Characters

### Separate characters
Your characters do not automatically share knowledge, relationships, enemies, criminal information, organization information, property, money, or possessions.

**Your characters do not share a brain, inventory, or bank account simply because you play all of them.**

### No self-transfers
Do not move money, vehicles, property, weapons, valuable items, business assets, or progression between your own characters, including through another player or deliberately placed items.

Legitimate inheritance may be approved when established beforehand through RP and appropriate IC documentation, such as a valid will created before death. It is not an automatic progression transfer.

### Conflicting characters
Characters may exist on opposing sides when otherwise permitted. Maintain strict separation of their knowledge and interests. Do not use alts to spy for yourself, protect your interests, sabotage opponents, or benefit another character you control.

### Character switching
Do not switch characters to escape active RP, consequences, debts, warrants, cooldowns, or character-limited systems.

### Original characters and backstories
Characters should be original. Inspiration is fine; directly portraying an existing celebrity, public figure, or established fictional character is not.

Backstory does not automatically grant mechanical wealth, property, government authority, ownership, skills, or special access.

### Names
Names may be unusual, but joke, meme, impersonation, slur, advertisement, or deliberately disruptive names may be rejected.

## 4. Conflict & Violence

### RDM
Do not attack or kill without reasonable RP justification. Violence should arise from story, not boredom.

### VDM
Do not use vehicles to randomly injure or kill players without legitimate RP justification.

### Escalation
Give conflict reasonable room to escalate. Existing feuds, threats, robberies, violent confrontations, or active pursuit may justify faster escalation.

**Context matters more than an artificial escalation checklist.**

### Revenge
Losing a confrontation does not automatically justify an immediate revenge hunt. Continuing retaliation should make sense within the story and your character's knowledge.

### Combat logging
Do not disconnect, respawn, switch characters, or use systems to escape active RP or its immediate consequences.

## 5. Crime, Police & Government

### Crime should create RP
Other players are not sources of money, equipment, or entertainment.

**Criminal RP should create conflict and stories—not maximize profit at everyone else's expense.**

### Crime has consequences
Criminal characters may be investigated, pursued, arrested, fined, jailed, or face other reasonable IC consequences. Legitimate consequences are not OOC punishment. Police and government characters likewise may not use IC authority to target players for OOC reasons.

### Law enforcement
A badge grants IC authority where Rosefire law permits it. It does not grant OOC authority or ownership of a scene.

### Evidence
Police may act on information reasonably obtained through RP and legitimate systems. OOC knowledge is not evidence. Mechanical data is not automatically IC-accessible information.

### Searches and arrests
Searches require reasonable IC justification. Police should not randomly search players hoping to find contraband.

If legitimately detained or arrested, participate in the RP rather than using mechanics or OOC arguments to escape it. Law enforcement should provide meaningful RP rather than abandoning restrained players.

### Jail
Sentences and fines should follow Rosefire's IC justice systems. Consequences should matter without unnecessarily removing someone from RP for excessive periods.

### Pursuits
Do not exploit glitches, broken terrain, teleportation, disconnecting, broken interiors, or unintended mechanics to guarantee escape or capture.

**Escaping police is allowed. Catching criminals is allowed. Neither side is entitled to win.**

### Surrender
A clear surrender should ordinarily end the immediate need for violence unless there is a strong RP reason otherwise. It does not erase the consequences that led to it.

### Corruption
Minor character flaws may develop naturally. Major ongoing corruption involving police, courts, government offices, public funds, evidence, armories, or shared institutional resources requires staff approval.

### Criminal organizations
Organization membership does not grant rule exemptions or permanent kill-on-sight permission. IC territorial claims do not create OOC ownership of public map areas or public systems.

## 6. Robbery, Kidnapping & Hostages

### Robbery is RP, not an inventory transfer
Give victims a reasonable opportunity to participate. Do not repeatedly target the same player because they are profitable or easy to rob.

Reasonable possessions may be demanded when they make sense in the situation, but winning does not entitle a robber to the victim's entire inventory.

Victims may not use OOC mechanics to hide possessions after being successfully robbed. Robbers may not use OOC inventory knowledge to demand hidden items their character has no reason to know about.

### Kidnapping
Kidnapping requires a reasonable RP purpose. Captives must still have opportunities to roleplay.

**Be considerate of the other player's time.** Do not keep someone restrained or isolated for an excessive period while providing little or no RP.

### Hostages
Hostages must be genuine participants. Do not use friends, alts, organization members, or willing accomplices as fake hostages to satisfy mechanics or guarantee favorable negotiations.

Police should reasonably value hostage lives. Criminals should recognize that harming hostages can escalate the response.

### Restraints
Restraints belong in active RP. Do not abandon someone restrained with no continuing RP simply to inconvenience them.

## 7. Downed, Medical & NLR

### Being downed
A mechanical down does not automatically mean death. Unless permanently killed, treat it as unconsciousness, incapacitation, or serious injury.

While downed, do not relay tactical information or continue participating in the confrontation.

**Being revived does not give you another life in the same fight.**

### Medical treatment
Allow reasonable medical RP when help is available. Mechanical revival does not instantly erase significant injuries.

Ongoing wounds recorded by Rosefire's medical systems should be reasonably acknowledged until treated or healed. The system supports continuity; it is not intended to force tedious recovery RP.

### Respawning
Respawning means your character was recovered and treated off-screen.

**Respawning removes your character from the active situation.**

Do not immediately return for equipment, revenge, arrests, or to help friends continue the same encounter.

### Memory
Being downed does not automatically erase memory. Characters may remember what they reasonably perceived before losing consciousness, but not information learned while unconscious.

### Returning
Once removed from an active conflict after being downed, your participation in that encounter is over. The story may continue later.

**NLR ends the encounter. It does not erase the story.**

## 8. Permanent Character Consequences

**Winning a scene does not give you ownership over someone else's character.**

Permanent scars, amputations, branding, tattoos, disfigurement, or comparable lasting changes require the affected player's OOC agreement. Temporary scene consequences do not.

Medical characters may diagnose and treat injuries, but major permanent physical changes should be collaborative.

### Permanent Character Death / CK
Mechanical incapacitation does not permanently kill a character. In most circumstances, the player decides when their character permanently dies.

Other players cannot declare a character permanently dead simply because they won a fight or believe an injury should have been fatal.

Exceptional forced-CK storylines require prior staff approval and the affected player must know beforehand that permanent death is a possible outcome.

Once deliberately declared permanently dead, the character remains dead.

## 9. Businesses, Organizations & Economy

Business and organization assets belong to the appropriate IC owner or organization. Mechanical access does not automatically mean IC ownership.

Do not empty shared resources for personal use or funnel progression between your own characters.

### Economy abuse
Do not knowingly exploit jobs, businesses, crafting, trading, NPCs, rewards, character slots, or broken systems for unintended gain.

**Finding a bug is not the problem. Deliberately abusing one is.**

Unexpected profit is not automatically exploitation. Knowledge and intent matter. Once you recognize a system is clearly malfunctioning, stop reproducing it and report it.

### Gifts
Reasonable IC gifts, loans, wages, supplies, and assistance are allowed. Do not use gifts to bypass progression or transfer wealth between your own characters.

### Real-money trading
Do not trade Rosefire characters, businesses, property, vehicles, items, currency, progression, or organization assets for real-world value outside systems officially approved by Rosefire.

## 10. Text, Voice & Accessibility

Voice and text are valid ways to roleplay. Do not harass, exclude, mock, or deliberately disadvantage someone because they communicate differently. Give text players reasonable time to respond.

**Accessibility needs are not fail-RP.**

Keep voice communication reasonably understandable. Deliberately disruptive audio, excessive soundboards, blasting music, or intentionally painful noise may result in staff intervention.

Voice changers and normal accessibility tools are allowed when they remain understandable and do not provide an unfair gameplay advantage.

## 11. Romance, Adult Content & Sensitive RP

### Romance
Romance between adult characters is allowed. IC romance creates no OOC obligation. Players may step away from romantic RP and may not be pressured into OOC contact or intimacy.

**IC relationships are between characters. OOC boundaries belong to the players.**

### ERP — hard prohibition
**ERP and explicit sexual roleplay are prohibited on Rosefire. Rosefire does not support an ERP system, channel, or approved ERP space. ERP is a bannable offense.**

This applies across FiveM and Rosefire-operated community spaces. Private in-game locations are not exceptions.

Adult intimacy in a story must **fade to black**. Characters may acknowledge afterward that something occurred without explicitly roleplaying it.

Nobody may receive gameplay advantages for sexual content or be disadvantaged for refusing it.

### Sexual violence
Sexual-violence RP is prohibited on Rosefire, including when participants claim OOC consent. It may not be actively roleplayed or graphically depicted.

### Sensitive content
Conflict may include intimidation, interrogation, captivity, and threats, but RP is not an excuse for prolonged degrading, sexualized, discriminatory, or personally targeted treatment.

Detailed torture or unusually graphic injury RP requires agreement from the affected player. Players may request that disturbing details be skipped.

### Pregnancy RP
Pregnancy RP is supported by Rosefire and may use Rosefire's pregnancy and family systems. Pregnancy may not be forced onto another character.

Major pregnancy-related story decisions affecting multiple characters should be communicated between the involved players.

**Abortion RP is not permitted on Rosefire.**

### Adult characters and children
Player-controlled characters must be adults. Child NPCs or dependents may exist through family systems, but are not playable characters and must remain completely separate from adult content.

### OOC boundaries
A player may ask for unusually sensitive content to stop, pause, or skip particular details. Respect that request.

This protects boundaries around **content**, not outcomes. It cannot be used to escape ordinary IC consequences such as arrest, robbery, losing a fight, or a successful investigation.

**A content boundary is not an outcome veto.**

## 12. Recording, Privacy & Technical Rules

Players may record or stream Rosefire. Streaming does not grant immunity from normal RP.

Recordings, screenshots, and logs may be submitted to staff as OOC evidence. Their existence does not automatically mean a character possesses an IC recording.

Do not expose another member's private or personally identifying information or redistribute private moderation material for retaliation, harassment, or public drama.

### AFK and technical problems
Briefly stepping away is understandable. Do not deliberately go AFK during active RP to avoid interaction or consequences.

Genuine crashes and hardware or connection failures are not automatically violations. Make a reasonable effort to return or communicate after being removed from active RP.

### Third-party software
Do not use cheats, injectors, unauthorized modifications, automation, macros, or external tools to gain an unfair gameplay advantage, reveal inaccessible information, manipulate mechanics, or bypass restrictions.

### Advertising and impersonation
Normal conversation about other communities is fine. Unsolicited promotion, mass DMs, recruitment campaigns, or joining primarily to redirect Rosefire members elsewhere is not.

**Rosefire is not a place for server bashing.** Do not use Rosefire spaces to insult, ridicule, talk down about, or stir up drama about other FiveM cities, their staff, their communities, or how they choose to run their servers. Discussing a past experience normally is fine; sustained negativity, comparison drama, or using Rosefire as a platform to attack another city is not.

**We are Rosefire. We are responsible for how Rosefire is run—not how another city chooses to operate.**

Do not impersonate Rosefire staff or falsely claim OOC staff authority.

## 13. Staff, Reports & Appeals

Staff status does not provide special IC authority. Staff tools or information may not benefit a staff member's character.

A report is a request to investigate, not proof. Provide context and evidence when available.

Staff should consider context, severity, intent, prior behavior, evidence, and impact. Not every mistake requires the same consequence.

Use Rosefire's designated support process for appeals. Harassing staff or repeatedly opening duplicate tickets is not an appeal.

## 14. When Something Goes Wrong

Do not turn an active scene into an OOC rules argument when it can reasonably be handled afterward. Finish or safely disengage where possible, preserve evidence, and use the support/report process.

Immediate safety concerns may always be brought directly to staff.

Staff may intervene in clearly exploitative, abusive, disruptive, or harmful conduct even when an exact case was not anticipated, but should explain what conduct was unacceptable and why.

---

## Final Principle

Rosefire's rules exist to protect collaborative roleplay and the people creating it.

**Use reasonable judgment. Respect other players. Protect the roleplay. Do not exploit technicalities.**

**We are here to create stories together.**
