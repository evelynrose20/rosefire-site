# Welcome to Paleto Bay

At the northern end of San Andreas, Paleto Bay sits between the Pacific and the slopes of Mount Chiliad. The highway passes through a town of small businesses, residential streets, and places where people stop on their way farther north or south.

The sea air and forested hills make it feel a long way from downtown Los Santos.

## Around town

Paleto Bay has the essentials close together. You'll find local shops, services, and quiet side streets a short distance from the main road. The town is small enough that familiar faces are common, though visitors are nothing unusual — especially on weekends.

There isn't much reason to rush through. Stop for a meal, walk around, and take a look at the view before getting back on the highway.

## The northern coast

The road along the water is one of the better drives in the state. There are stretches of open coastline, bends with views of the ocean, and places to pull off when the light is right. Morning fog isn't unusual, and the weather can feel cooler than it did farther south.

The coast has long been a route for people traveling between smaller communities. That still shows in the roadside businesses and the steady movement of vehicles through town.

## Life up north

People here are used to planning around the distance to larger services and shops. Neighbors matter, especially when somebody needs help on a quiet road. You'll hear the usual small-town disagreements about growth, newcomers, and what ought to stay the same.

None of that means the town is stuck in the past. It has its own businesses and plans for the future, like anywhere else.

## Out for the day

Paleto Bay makes a good base for visiting [[regions/wilderness|Mount Chiliad]] and nearby forest roads. Or follow [[regions/coast|the coast]] south and watch the landscape change along the way.
