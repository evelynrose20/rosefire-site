# Emergency Medical Services & Fire

Emergency medical and fire services assist residents facing serious injuries, sudden illness, fires, and other emergencies. Their work includes responding to calls and helping people stay safe before a situation becomes critical.

## Medical Emergencies

Seek urgent medical help when someone is seriously injured, has difficulty breathing, becomes unconscious, or shows signs of a medical emergency. Give dispatchers your location and as much useful information as you can.

## Fire & Rescue

Report fires and dangerous conditions promptly. Keep access roads and building entrances clear for responding crews, and do not return to an unsafe area until emergency personnel say it is safe.

## Seasonal Safety

Cold weather brings its own risks. Residents spending time outdoors during the snow season should dress appropriately and take symptoms of cold exposure seriously. See the [[city-guide/winter|winter safety notices]] for additional guidance.
