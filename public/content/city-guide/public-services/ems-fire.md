# Medical Care & Emergency Response

Emergency Medical Services and Fire personnel respond to urgent medical calls, fires, and other dangerous incidents across San Andreas. Their work is focused on helping people in need and keeping emergency scenes safe.

## When Someone Needs Medical Help

Seek emergency assistance for serious injuries, difficulty breathing, unconsciousness, or other urgent symptoms. Tell the dispatcher where you are and what assistance is needed.

## Fire & Rescue Services

Report fires and hazardous conditions promptly. Keep access routes open for emergency vehicles and do not enter an unsafe building or incident area.

## Health & Seasonal Safety

Cold weather brings increased risks for people spending long periods outdoors. Dress warmly, get out of wet clothing, and take signs of cold exposure seriously. Visit the [[city-guide/winter|winter safety information]] for guidance.
