# Garbage Collection

Los Santos Sanitation keeps the streets moving every day. This guide covers a sanitation shift from the moment you arrive at the depot until your pay reaches Fleeca.

## Before Your First Shift

Sanitation work starts at the **Los Santos Sanitation depot**. Look for Los Santos Sanitation on your map and enter the office marker to open your employment dashboard.

You need a sanitation truck registered in your character's name. Bring the correct truck to the depot before clocking in. The office will not dispatch you in somebody else's truck or in a truck that does not match the assignment you selected.

Your driver's license must also cover the truck you want to operate.

| Assignment | Available | License | Base Hopper |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neighborhood Refuse Truck | Sanitation Level 1 | Class D | 8 bags |
| City Refuse Truck | Sanitation Level 5 | CDL Class B | 14 bags |
| Heavy Refuse Truck | Sanitation Level 20 | CDL Class A | 20 bags |

A higher license class also clears you for assignments below it.

## Starting Work

1. Bring your registered sanitation truck to the depot.
2. Walk into the sanitation office marker and press **E**.
3. Review your sanitation record, available routes, truck assignments, licenses, and skills.
4. Choose an assignment your sanitation level and driver's license allow.
5. Choose an available route and clock in.
6. Your work uniform will be issued and Dispatch will mark your first collection stop on the map.

Keep your assigned truck nearby while collecting. Dispatch will not authorize a pickup if the truck has been left too far from the collection point.

## Following Your Route

Your GPS marks the active sanitation stop. At each stop, approach the collection marker on foot and press **E** when prompted to **Collect Refuse**.

Some collection points may require more than one bag. Keep working the same stop until Dispatch advances you to the next one.

Once a bag is in your hands:

1. Return to the rear of your sanitation truck.
2. Move close to the hopper.
3. Press **E** when prompted to **Load Refuse Into Truck**.
4. The bag is added to the hopper and your route record advances.

While sitting in your assigned truck, the sanitation display shows your **stops remaining, current hopper load, hopper capacity, and current shift pay**. A smaller hopper display is also visible near the rear of the truck while you are working around it.

## Hopper Capacity & Compaction

Every sanitation truck has a hopper limit. When the hopper fills, what happens next depends on the truck.

The **Neighborhood Refuse Truck** has no compaction cycle. Once it reaches capacity, return to the sanitation transfer point and empty it.

The **City Refuse Truck** has one compaction cycle. When its hopper is full, go to the rear of the truck and press **E** when prompted to **Run Compactor**. A successful cycle gives the truck additional working capacity.

The **Heavy Refuse Truck** has two compaction cycles and can stay on the road longer before it must be emptied.

After all available compaction cycles have been used and the hopper reaches its current limit, Dispatch will route you back to the sanitation transfer point.

## Emptying the Truck

When the truck needs to be emptied, your route marker changes to the sanitation transfer point.

Drive the assigned truck into the transfer area, approach the marked point, and press **E** when prompted to **Empty Sanitation Truck**. The hopper is cleared, its normal capacity is restored, and its compaction cycles are reset.

Dispatch will then send you back to your active route.

## Completing Routes

When the final collection point is finished, the route is recorded as complete and the route-completion pay is added to your running shift total.

If you remain clocked in, Dispatch can immediately issue another eligible route. You do not have to return to the office between completed routes.

Sanitation experience is earned while you work:

- **8 XP** for every bag successfully loaded.
- **80 XP** for every completed route.
- Sanitation rank runs from **Level 1 through Level 20**.
- A skill point is earned every three sanitation levels after Level 1.

Your sanitation record keeps track of bags collected, routes completed, unusual finds, career earnings, attendance marks, and suspensions.

## Skills

Skill points can be spent from the sanitation office while you are off shift.

### Quick Hands

Reduces the time it takes to collect a refuse bag. Quick Hands has three ranks.

### Sharp Eye

Improves your chance of noticing unusual discarded items while working. Sharp Eye has three ranks.

### Efficient Loader

Improves loading and compaction efficiency. It increases working hopper capacity and improves compactor performance. Efficient Loader has three ranks.

## Unusual Finds

Every now and then, something worth remembering survives among the refuse. If you discover an unusual item, Dispatch will let you know that you are cleared to keep it.

These discoveries are recorded with your sanitation career and can contribute to sanitation achievements and collections.

## Pay

Sanitation pay builds into a running shift total while you work.

- **$125** is added for each bag successfully loaded.
- **$750** is added for completing a route.
- **$150 active-duty payroll** is added every 15 minutes while you are actively working.

Your current running total is visible on the sanitation display while you are in the truck.

When you clock out, Los Santos Sanitation sends the accumulated shift pay to your **Fleeca account**. You will receive confirmation of the payroll deposit when the shift closes.

## Clocking Out

When you are ready to finish:

1. Return to the Los Santos Sanitation office.
2. Open the sanitation dashboard.
3. Clock out.
4. Your work uniform is removed and your previous clothing is restored.
5. Your accumulated shift pay is deposited through Fleeca.

You can finish after one route or remain on duty for additional assignments.

## Attendance

Do not remain clocked in and abandon an active shift.

Dispatch checks for work activity. After an extended period without route activity, you will first receive an attendance warning and then a final warning. Continued inactivity can place an attendance tally on your sanitation record.

Repeated attendance tallies can temporarily suspend your sanitation work privileges.

If you are finished working, clock out rather than leaving the shift running.

## If Your Shift Will Not Start

Check these before contacting city staff:

- Your sanitation truck is at the depot and close to you.
- The truck is registered in your character's name.
- The truck model matches the assignment you selected.
- Your sanitation level is high enough for that assignment.
- Your driver's license or CDL class covers the selected truck.
- Your sanitation work privileges are not currently suspended.

The sanitation dashboard shows your available assignments, license clearance, progression, skills, routes, and employment record.

## Quick Shift Checklist

**Arrive with your truck → Open the sanitation office → Select an eligible assignment and route → Clock in → Follow the GPS → Collect refuse → Load bags at the rear of the truck → Compact or empty the hopper when required → Complete the route → Continue working or return to the office → Clock out → Fleeca receives your shift pay.**

Keep the truck close, follow Dispatch, and keep Los Santos clean.
