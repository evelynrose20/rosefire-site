# Employment Across the State

A working day looks different from one part of San Andreas to another. There are collection trucks to dispatch, deliveries to make, residents to serve, and local businesses to run. Some work begins at a depot; other opportunities start with a conversation or a visit to an employer's office.

Find a starting point below, and speak to the relevant employer about current requirements.

## Public Service Careers

Interested in joining a department? The Police Department and EMS & Fire provide information about recruitment through their department websites **inside San Andreas**. Visit a public library or use a laptop or desktop computer to find the relevant department website and see whether applications are being accepted.

The Department of Transportation provides roadside and recovery services; its public information page is available below.

## Local Businesses & Employment

Restaurants, shops, service companies, and other local employers may advertise opportunities or recruit directly. Ask at the business, check local notices, or look for vacancy announcements through the city's computer services.

Each employer sets its own hiring requirements, duties, and working arrangements. Not every business is hiring at all times.

## Finding Work

For sanitation work, speak to Los Santos Sanitation and review its vehicle and licensing requirements. Propane delivery begins with Jonny Shapiro Gas Specialist and requires a suitable registered service truck.

For department positions or openings with local employers, use the employment and department websites available on a library computer, laptop, or desktop within San Andreas. Confirm requirements with the employer before making arrangements.
