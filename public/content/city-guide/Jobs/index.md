# Jobs & Careers

Rosefire has multiple ways to earn a living.

You can work a city-provided job on your own, build a career in public service, work for another player's business, or develop a profession of your own.

Choose the kind of work you're looking for below.

## City Jobs

City jobs are available without needing another player to employ you.

These are designed to remain playable even when the city is quiet and often include their own routes, equipment, progression, or career systems.

- [[Garbage Collection]]
- [[Propane Delivery]]
- [[Bus Driver]]
- [[Trucking]]
- [[Taxi]]

## Public Service

Public-service careers are structured departments with ranks, responsibilities, and roleplay expectations.

- [[Police Department]]
- [[EMS]]
- [[DOJ]]

These are careers rather than quick money-making activities and may require applications, training, or certification.

## Player-Owned Businesses

Many Rosefire businesses are owned and operated by players.

Business owners control their own staff, wages, schedules, and day-to-day operations.

Possible employment includes:

- Restaurants
- Nightclubs
- Arcades
- Gas Stations
- Factories
- Retail businesses
- Other player-owned companies

## Professions

Some activities can be enjoyed casually or developed into a source of income.

- [[Fishing]]
- [[Hunting]]
- [[Mining]]
- [[Farming]]

These systems may include progression, contracts, gathering, crafting, or changing market demand.

## Finding Work

Available work can vary depending on the type of job.

City jobs can generally be started directly through their appropriate workplace or supervisor.

Player-owned businesses may advertise openings through Rosefire's Jobs systems or hire residents directly.

Public-service departments may require an application and approval before you can begin working.

> You do not need another player online just to have something productive to do in Rosefire.
