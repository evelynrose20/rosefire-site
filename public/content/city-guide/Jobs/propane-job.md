# Propane Delivery

Jonny Shapiro Gas Specialist keeps propane tanks supplied across Los Santos and Blaine County. This guide covers the full job, from preparing your service truck to loading propane, completing deliveries, and building your propane career.

## Before Your First Shift

Propane delivery uses your own **Brute Utility Truck (Cargo)**. The service truck can be purchased through **Los Santos Commercial & Fleet**.

Bring your registered service truck when starting work. Dispatch verifies that the truck belongs to your character before assigning a route.

Your truck has its own persistent propane supply. Propane left in the truck stays with that vehicle between shifts.

## Starting Work

1. Bring your registered Brute Utility Truck to Jonny Shapiro Gas Specialist.
2. Open the propane dispatch terminal.
3. Review your propane career and available work.
4. Choose an available service area or route.
5. Clock in with your service truck nearby.
6. Your Public Worker uniform will be issued and Dispatch will mark your first customer tank.

Your original clothing is restored when the shift ends.

## Loading Propane

Your service truck must have propane on board before it can supply customer tanks.

Drive to **Propane Bulk Supply**, marked on the map. The bulk station uses its own commercial loading terminal rather than the job dispatch screen.

1. Park the service truck within reach of the yard supply hose.
2. Take the **Yard Supply Hose**.
3. Connect it to the service truck.
4. Choose how much propane you want to purchase.
5. Pay with cash or an authorized Fleeca debit card.
6. Complete the load and return the supply hose.

The terminal shows the current wholesale price. Your truck cannot be loaded beyond its service-tank capacity.

## Following Your Route

Dispatch builds work around customer tanks that need service. Choose a service area and Dispatch will build an available delivery route.

Your GPS marks the current customer tank. Bring the service truck close enough for the delivery hose to reach before getting out.

While sitting in the truck, the route tracker shows your **current stop, total stops, propane remaining, service-tank capacity, current earnings, and delivery status**.

### Moving the Route Tracker

Press **F7** while the tracker is visible to enter Move HUD mode.

Drag the tracker wherever you prefer, then press **Escape** to finish. Your position is remembered for future sessions.

## Making a Delivery

1. Park within hose range of the marked customer tank.
2. Go to the hose connection on the service truck.
3. Press **E** to **Take Propane Hose**.
4. Carry the hose to the customer tank.
5. Press **E** to **Connect Hose**.
6. Press **E** to **Start Delivery**.
7. Allow the required propane to transfer.
8. Disconnect the hose when the delivery finishes.
9. Return to the truck and **Return Propane Hose**.
10. Close the delivery ticket when prompted.

The truck loses the amount actually delivered and the customer tank gains that amount. Partial deliveries only pay for propane successfully transferred.

The delivery hose is only available when the truck is close enough to the active customer stop. If you need to cancel before connecting, return to the truck and use **Return Propane Hose**.

## Pay

Propane earnings build as you complete customer deliveries and routes. Payment is based on propane actually delivered, with additional stop and route bonuses.

Your running earnings appear on the in-truck tracker. Completed payments are deposited into your personal **Fleeca account**, with Fleeca activity delivered through the phone notification system.

## Service Truck Capacity

A new service truck begins with a **1,000 gallon** propane capacity.

The **Load Management** skill increases capacity by 100 gallons per rank, up to an additional 300 gallons.

Propane stock belongs to the vehicle, so you can return to the bulk station when needed and continue working with the same truck.

## Career Progression

Propane has its own career progression from **Level 1 through Level 20**.

- **25 XP** for completing a customer stop.
- **100 XP** for completing a route.
- A skill point every three propane levels after Level 1.

Your career records gallons delivered, stops completed, routes completed, career earnings, level, experience, and unlocked skills.

## Skills

### Hose Handling

Increases propane transfer speed by 12% per rank. Three ranks are available.

### Load Management

Adds 100 gallons of service-truck capacity per rank. Three ranks are available.

### Customer Service

Increases the customer-stop bonus by 8% per rank. Three ranks are available.

## Achievements

Propane work contributes to Employment achievements, including your first stop and route, lifetime gallon milestones, route milestones, Propane Level 10, and mastering every propane skill.

## Clocking Out

End your propane shift through Dispatch when you are finished.

Your Public Worker uniform is removed and your previous clothing is restored. Propane remaining in your registered service truck stays with the vehicle for your next shift.

## If Your Shift Will Not Start

- Make sure you are using the correct Brute Utility Truck (Cargo).
- Make sure the service truck is registered to your character.
- Keep the truck close enough for Dispatch to verify it.
- Select an available service area or route.
- The selected area must have customer tanks requiring service.

If your truck is low on propane, visit Propane Bulk Supply before continuing deliveries.

## Quick Shift Checklist

**Bring your registered service truck → Open Propane Dispatch → Choose a service area → Clock in → Load propane if needed → Follow the GPS → Park within hose range → Take the truck hose → Connect to the customer tank → Start delivery → Disconnect → Return the hose → Close the delivery ticket → Continue the route → Clock out when finished.**

Keep the service truck supplied, return your equipment after every stop, and keep Rosefire's propane customers running.
