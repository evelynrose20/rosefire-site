# Player-Owned Businesses

Rosefire has businesses that can be owned, managed, and staffed by players.

You can work for an existing business, purchase one of the city's established business types, or work toward having a custom business added to Rosefire.

This section explains the OOC systems players need in order to understand employment and ownership. Individual hiring decisions, internal business rules, prices, strategies, and day-to-day operations should still be handled through RP.
