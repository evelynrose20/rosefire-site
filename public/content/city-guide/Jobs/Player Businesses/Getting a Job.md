# Getting a Job at a Player-Owned Business

Player-owned businesses are run by actual players rather than the city.

That means getting hired works differently from a normal city job.

You do not simply walk up to a marker, clock in, and start working.

Each business decides who it hires, what positions are available, what employees are paid, and how its staff is managed.

## Finding Businesses That Are Hiring

Player businesses can choose whether they are currently hiring.

When a business is hiring, it may appear through Rosefire's job or business systems.

You may also learn about openings through:

- Business advertisements
- Word of mouth
- In-character conversations
- Business websites or listings
- Direct contact with the owner or management
- Other employees

Not every business will always be hiring.

## Applying for a Job

How you apply depends on the business.

Some businesses may use a formal application process.

Others may prefer to speak with you directly in character.

A business may ask for things such as:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- Your email
- Your availability
- Previous experience
- Why your character wants the position

The exact process is up to that business.

## Interviews

Some owners or managers may want to interview you before hiring you.

This is part of the roleplay.

An interview may be simple or more detailed depending on the business and position.

You are not guaranteed a job simply because you applied.

## Getting Hired

If a business decides to hire you, the owner or someone with the correct management permissions can add you to the business staff.

Once hired, the business may assign you:

- A job
- A rank
- A position
- A wage
- Business permissions

Different businesses may have different rank structures.

## Clocking In

Player businesses can use Rosefire's job systems to manage employees.

Depending on the business, employees may need to clock in before they can use certain work systems.

A business can also control whether it is currently open or closed.

If the business is closed, employees may be prevented from clocking in until it is opened again.

## Your Active Job

Rosefire allows characters to hold multiple jobs.

You can switch which job is currently active through the Jobs app.

Only your active job is used for systems that require you to be working under a specific employer.

## Employee Pay

Player-owned businesses pay employees from the business's own funds.

The server does not create payroll money for a player-owned business.

If the business does not have enough money available for payroll, it cannot simply pay employees with money that does not exist.

Business owners are responsible for deciding:

- Employee wages
- Which positions receive which pay
- How many employees the business can afford
- How the business manages its payroll

## Working Hours

Rosefire tracks employee work time for supported player businesses.

This allows owners and managers to see employee activity and helps businesses keep track of who has been working.

Depending on the business, your pay may be connected to the amount of time you are clocked in.

## Direct Deposit

If supported by the business and your banking setup, wages can be sent directly to your checking account.

Direct deposit settings can be managed through Rosefire's banking and job systems.

## Business Rules

Every player-owned business can have its own internal rules.

For example, an owner may decide:

- Uniform requirements
- Employee duties
- Opening procedures
- Closing procedures
- Training requirements
- Rank responsibilities
- Scheduling expectations
- Employee conduct rules

These are business decisions and should usually be learned through RP.

## Leaving a Business

If you no longer want to work for a business, you can leave the position through the appropriate job systems or speak with the business owner or management.

Owners and managers can also remove employees from their staff.

## Good to Know

- Player businesses choose who they hire.
- Hiring is not automatic.
- Businesses control their own wages.
- Employee pay comes from actual business funds.
- Businesses may use ranks and permissions.
- Some work systems require you to be clocked in.
- Businesses can open or close their operations.
- Rosefire supports multiple jobs per character.
- You can switch your active job through the Jobs app.
- Business-specific rules should generally be learned through RP.

This guide explains the OOC mechanics needed to understand player employment.

Individual hiring standards, workplace culture, pay decisions, and internal procedures belong to the business itself.
