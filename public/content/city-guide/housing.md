# Housing in San Andreas

A new address is an important decision. Apartments, houses, and other properties are found throughout San Andreas, with different surroundings and arrangements to suit residents looking for somewhere to settle.

## Finding a Property

**D8** can help residents enquire about properties available to buy or lease. Ask about viewings, prices, and current availability before making arrangements. Listings can change, so confirm details with the agency.

## Renting & Leasing

A lease sets out the terms of occupying a property, including rent and the agreed period. Read the terms carefully and ask about costs before signing.

## Buying a Home

Buying a property can involve a deposit and financing. Your bank can provide information about mortgage arrangements, repayments, and applicable rates. Speak with D8 about the sale and property details.

## Property Records & Enquiries

Keep copies of any signed lease or purchase documents. If you have questions about an existing property arrangement, contact the relevant agency or lender.
