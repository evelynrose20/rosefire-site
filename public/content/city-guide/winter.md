# Winter in San Andreas

From November 1 through March 31, snow covers the state. This is San Andreas's winter season, from the streets of Los Santos to the roads of Blaine County and the forests of the north.

## Traveling During Winter

Expect snow-covered roads across the state throughout the season. Allow more time for longer journeys and drive carefully, particularly on rural roads and mountain routes. Falling snow and visibility can still change from day to day.

## See the State in Winter

Los Santos looks different beneath the snow, while Mount Chiliad and the northern forests are especially striking. The Alamo Sea and desert roads offer a less familiar view of the season.

For destination ideas, see [[explore|Explore San Andreas]].

## The Holiday Calendar

Winter includes the December holidays, New Year's Day, and Valentine's Day. You can see the complete year in the [[city-guide/celebrations|annual celebrations calendar]].

The snow season ends on March 31, with the next season beginning April 1.
