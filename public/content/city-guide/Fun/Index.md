# Things to Do Across San Andreas

Looking for somewhere to spend an afternoon or make a night of it? From the golf course in Los Santos to the casino floor at the Diamond, there's plenty to enjoy around the state.

## A Day Out

### Los Santos Golf Club

Leave the city traffic behind for a round of golf. The [[city-guide/Fun/Los santos Golf Club|Los Santos Golf Club]] is a familiar stop for anyone who prefers a quieter afternoon on the fairways.

### The Diamond Casino & Resort

Planning a weekend in Los Santos? The [[city-guide/Fun/Diamond Casino|Diamond Casino & Resort]] offers gaming tables, slots, racing, and places to meet friends. Check the visiting hours before making the trip.

## Around Town

San Andreas has more to offer after sunset. Keep an eye out for local nightclubs, arcades, and other entertainment around Los Santos. What's open and where people gather can change from one neighborhood to the next.

## Out in the Open

A day off doesn't have to mean staying in town. The coast, the Alamo Sea, and the northern mountains are all worth a visit. Bring your fishing gear if you're heading for the water, or take the long way home and see what you find.

For places to visit outside the city, browse [[explore|Explore San Andreas]].

## Through the Year

Holidays bring their own traditions. Look out for Halloween celebrations in October and Christmas festivities toward the end of the year. Local announcements are the best place to find out what's happening.
