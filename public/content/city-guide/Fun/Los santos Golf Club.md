# Los Santos Golf Club

![[Pasted image 20261004061402.png]]

For an afternoon away from the busy streets, visit the Los Santos Golf Club. The fairways offer a quieter side of the city and a good reason to spend a few hours outdoors.

It's a familiar name among golfers in Los Santos, whether you're making a visit for the course or simply enjoying the surroundings.
