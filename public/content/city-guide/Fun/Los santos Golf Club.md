# Los Santos Golf Club

![[Pasted image 20261004061402.png]]

Los Santos Golf Club is welcoming golfers back for a little time on the fairways, even as work continues around the property.

## Nine Holes Available

Visitors can currently enjoy a **nine-hole round of golf**. The club is operating on a limited basis while the remaining course and other facilities receive attention.

It's still a fine place to get out of the city for an afternoon, whether you're a regular golfer or just fancy trying a round.

## A Message from Cait, Club Owner

Club owner **Cait** hopes to welcome longtime members and new faces alike when the club fully reopens. Plans include opening the remaining holes and introducing more games and activities around the property.

## Looking Ahead

Cait hopes to hold a fuller reopening in **summer 2027**, with the remaining holes and additional games and activities available to members and newcomers.

The date is **a goal, not a confirmed opening announcement**. For now, the nine-hole course remains the club's available offering.
