# Annual Celebrations in San Andreas

Each holiday has its own traditions, with seasonal announcements published as plans are confirmed.

## January
> New Year's celebrations carry on into the first days of January, with winter snow across San Andreas.
- New Year's celebrations — through January 9
- Activity: New Year's Advent Calendar

## February
> February is for Valentine's traditions, little gifts, and time spent with the people who matter.
- Valentine's Month — February 1–28/29
- Activity: Valentine's Advent Calendar

## March
> Snow stays on the roads and rooftops through the final month of winter.
- Winter season ends March 31

## April
> Spring arrives, and Easter celebrations last all month.
- Easter Month — April 1–30
- Activity: Easter Advent Calendar

## May
> Longer days and milder weather make it a good month to visit the countryside.
- Spring outings and recreation

## June
> The first month of summer is a good time to visit the coast.
- Summer in San Andreas

## July
> Make the most of long days, beaches, and the roads beyond Los Santos.
- Summer outings and coastal travel

## August
> There's still time to spend a summer afternoon by the water or in the mountains.
- Summer activities around the state

## September
> September marks Rosefire's anniversary, a month set aside to celebrate the community and the state it calls home.
- Rosefire Anniversary Month — September 1–30
- Activity: Anniversary Advent Calendar

## October
> October brings costumes, candy, and Halloween traditions across San Andreas.
- Halloween Month — October 1–31
- Activity: Halloween Advent Calendar
- Activity: Trick or Treat
- Activity: Treat or Shock Bags

## November
> Snow returns to San Andreas on November 1, alongside a month of Thanksgiving traditions.
- Thanksgiving Month — November 1–30
- Winter snow season begins November 1
- Activity: Thanksgiving Advent Calendar

## December
> Christmas fills the first part of December, before New Year's celebrations take over from the 26th.
- Christmas season — December 1–25
- New Year's celebrations begin December 26
- Winter snow continues
- Activity: Christmas Advent Calendar
- Activity: New Year's Advent Calendar
