# Rosefire Penal Code

**STATE OF SAN ANDREAS — DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE**

**Version 1.1 — Sections 1–2**

**Status: ENACTED — IN FORCE**

An Act defining criminal offenses, establishing criminal classifications and authorized penalties, and providing for the consistent enforcement of criminal law throughout the State of San Andreas.

Classification of criminal offenses, general penalty schedules, liability, attempts, accomplices, conspiracy, sentencing authority, fines, restitution, and official records.
