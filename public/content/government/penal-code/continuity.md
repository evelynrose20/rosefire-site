# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. The Rosefire Penal Code shall be enforced consistently with the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

2. Fundamental civil rights shall apply automatically to every accused individual.

3. No criminal accusation shall independently establish guilt.

4. Fines and imprisonment shall remain subject to the applicable statutory limits.

5. The statutory maximum of seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 hours, for fixed-term imprisonment shall remain in force.

6. A sentence exceeding six hours shall require an express statutory basis and appropriate judicial determination.

7. Life imprisonment shall require the applicable human judicial proceedings.

8. Capital punishment shall require satisfaction of the exceptional eligibility and procedural requirements established by law.

9. Escape from prison shall not independently establish eligibility for capital punishment.

10. Police, prosecutors, correctional personnel, and judicial authorities shall not impose criminal penalties outside their lawful powers.

11. Nothing in this Code shall authorize roleplay expressly prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

12. Enforcement of out-of-character community standards shall remain separate from in-character criminal prosecution.

13. The official Penal Code may be amended through lawful state procedures, with changes identified by revision and effective date.

14. Existing provisions shall remain in force unless amended, repealed, or superseded through lawful authority.
