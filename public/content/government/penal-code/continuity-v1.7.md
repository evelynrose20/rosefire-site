# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 13–14 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–12 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. All rights established by the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code shall remain applicable.

3. Fraud and forgery offenses shall require proof of the applicable material deception, falsification, and criminal intent.

4. The possession of an inaccurate document shall not independently establish knowing criminal forgery.

5. Ordinary aliases, protected expression, and lawful professional activity shall not be criminalized without a separate statutory basis.

6. Warrants, criminal charges, arrests, and searches shall remain subject to legally established requirements.

7. The prosecution shall establish criminal guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.

8. Sentencing enhancements shall require legal authorization and supporting evidence.

9. No enhancement shall automatically override an offense's maximum penalty.

10. Exceptional fixed-term sentences exceeding six hours shall require express statutory authority and appropriate judicial determination.

11. The maximum fixed-term sentence shall remain seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 elapsed hours, for a single sentencing event.

12. Life imprisonment shall remain separate from fixed-term imprisonment and require authorized human judicial determination.

13. Capital punishment shall remain subject to the prior-life-sentence requirement, a subsequent independently life-eligible criminal offense, and separate human judicial sentencing.

14. The Judicial Intelligence Service shall not independently impose life imprisonment or capital punishment.

15. Unauthorized alteration of judicial records shall remain subject to applicable criminal law.

16. No criminal provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

17. Where multiple provisions punish the same criminal act, applicable merger and double-jeopardy protections shall remain controlling.

18. Sections 13–14 remain enacted and in force unless amended or repealed through lawful authority.
