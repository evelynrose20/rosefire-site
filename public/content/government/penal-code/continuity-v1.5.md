# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 9–10 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–8 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. Wildlife conservation, hunting, and fishing regulation shall remain governed by Civil Rights & Justice Code Section 14.

3. Aviation and maritime regulation shall remain governed by Civil Rights & Justice Code Section 15.

4. Territorial boundaries and restricted geographic areas shall remain governed by Civil Rights & Justice Code Section 17.

5. Lawful hunting, fishing, aviation, and maritime activity shall not independently establish criminal suspicion.

6. Official conservation schedules shall identify protected species, hunting seasons, fishing restrictions, and other relevant classifications.

7. Official aviation and maritime schedules shall identify applicable aircraft classifications, licensing requirements, restricted airspace, navigational restrictions, and other relevant regulations.

8. No officer shall independently create a protected species, prohibited hunting season, restricted airspace, or other criminal restriction without lawful authority.

9. Searches, seizures, detention, arrest, and enforcement shall comply with applicable civil rights protections.

10. Administrative licensing actions shall remain separate from criminal sentencing.

11. Criminal fines shall remain within their authorized statutory ranges.

12. Restitution and other lawful civil remedies may be ordered separately where appropriate.

13. Sentences exceeding six hours shall require express statutory authorization and appropriate judicial determination.

14. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 hours, for a single sentencing event.

15. Life imprisonment shall require a qualifying offense and authorized human judicial proceedings.

16. Capital punishment shall remain subject to the exceptional eligibility and procedural safeguards established under the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

17. No provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

18. Where multiple offenses describe the same criminal conduct, applicable merger and double-jeopardy protections shall govern.

19. These provisions remain enacted and in force unless amended or repealed through lawful state authority.
