# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 15–16 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–14 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. All criminal proceedings shall remain subject to the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

3. Public safety and emergency response authority shall not override fundamental civil rights.

4. Emergency personnel shall retain applicable legal protections while performing authorized duties.

5. Commercial waste, hazardous-material, environmental, and public utility regulations shall require an effective legal basis before criminal penalties may be imposed.

6. Correctional custody shall require lawful authority.

7. No correctional officer shall independently impose or extend a judicial sentence.

8. Fixed-term sentence completion shall not depend on an officer being physically present.

9. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 elapsed hours, for a single sentencing event.

10. Life imprisonment shall remain separate from fixed-term imprisonment and shall require human judicial authorization.

11. Capital punishment shall require the exceptional eligibility and procedures established under the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

12. Escape from correctional custody shall not independently authorize capital punishment.

13. The Judicial Intelligence Service shall not independently impose life imprisonment or capital punishment.

14. Correctional record alteration shall remain subject to criminal investigation and judicial review.

15. Lawfully issued and authenticated custody records shall remain enforceable according to their legal effect.

16. A mere unsubstantiated allegation of record manipulation shall not authorize indefinite detention.

17. Criminal fines shall remain within their applicable statutory ranges.

18. Restitution, property recovery, and environmental remediation shall remain separate from criminal fines.

19. Sentences exceeding six hours shall require express statutory authority and appropriate judicial determination.

20. Offenses based on identical conduct shall remain subject to applicable merger and double-jeopardy protections.

21. No provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

22. These provisions remain enacted and in force unless amended or repealed through lawful state authority.
