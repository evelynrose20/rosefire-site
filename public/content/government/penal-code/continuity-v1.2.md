# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 3–4 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–2 of the Rosefire Penal Code and the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

2. All criminal offenses shall require proof of their applicable elements.

3. Accusations, prior arrests, or association with suspected criminals shall not independently establish guilt.

4. Lawful property ownership, contractual disputes, and civil disagreements shall not automatically constitute criminal wrongdoing.

5. Criminal fines shall remain within their applicable statutory ranges.

6. Restitution may be ordered separately for reasonably established loss.

7. Ordinary fixed-term sentences shall remain subject to the classification schedules established under Penal Code §1.3.

8. Exceptional and extraordinary fixed-term sentences shall require express offense-specific authorization.

9. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 hours, for a single sentencing event.

10. Life imprisonment shall require a qualifying offense and human judicial determination.

11. Capital punishment shall not be imposed automatically for a Capital Felony.

12. All capital sentencing shall comply with the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

13. Arrest, search, seizure, forfeiture, and criminal processing shall remain subject to applicable civil rights and legal procedures.

14. No provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

15. Where multiple offense provisions describe the same criminal act, the applicable legal rules concerning merger and duplicative punishment shall govern.

16. Official property-value thresholds and relevant financial offense classifications shall be maintained in duly adopted schedules.

17. These provisions remain in force unless amended or repealed through lawful authority.
