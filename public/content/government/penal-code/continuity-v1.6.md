# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 11–12 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–10 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. Banking, property, mortgage, foreclosure, and commercial protections shall remain governed by Section 16 of the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

3. Official judicial records and the Judicial Intelligence Service shall remain governed by the applicable judicial and criminal provisions.

4. Computer-related criminal offenses shall apply to unauthorized conduct rather than any particular technological device, implant, or professional identity.

5. No specialized cyberware classification, datarunner license, or cybernetic equipment restriction is established by these provisions.

6. Lawful technology development, authorized system maintenance, cybersecurity testing, and ordinary electronic communication shall remain protected within their legal scope.

7. Financial crime allegations shall not independently authorize unrestricted access to private banking records.

8. Criminal organizations shall be defined by established prohibited conduct, not merely by membership or reputation.

9. Organized crime penalties shall not be imposed solely because multiple individuals know one another or operate a lawful business together.

10. All criminal fines shall remain subject to the applicable statutory ranges.

11. Restitution and civil financial remedies shall remain distinct from criminal penalties.

12. Sentences exceeding six hours shall require express statutory authority and appropriate judicial determination.

13. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 hours, for a single sentencing event.

14. Life imprisonment shall require a separately qualifying capital offense and an authorized human judicial proceeding.

15. Capital punishment shall remain subject to the extraordinary eligibility and procedural safeguards established by the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

16. Arrests, searches, seizures, financial account restrictions, and property forfeitures shall remain subject to applicable civil rights protections.

17. Multiple offenses arising from identical conduct shall remain subject to the applicable rules against duplicative punishment.

18. No provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

19. These provisions remain enacted and in force unless amended or repealed through lawful state authority.
