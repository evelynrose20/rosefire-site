# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 5–6 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–4 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. All criminal enforcement shall comply with the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

3. Traffic citations shall not independently authorize arrest or vehicle searches beyond the powers expressly granted by law.

4. Commercial vehicle and DOT enforcement shall remain subject to the protections established under Sections 10–11 of the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

5. Lawful refusal to consent to a search, protected silence, and requests for counsel shall not constitute obstruction.

6. Criminal penalties shall remain within the applicable offense-specific limits.

7. Restitution and authorized administrative licensing consequences shall remain distinct from criminal fines.

8. Exceptional and extraordinary sentences exceeding six hours shall require expressly established legal authority and the required judicial determination.

9. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days for a single sentencing event.

10. Life imprisonment and capital punishment shall remain restricted to qualifying offenses and authorized human judicial proceedings.

11. Escape from custody shall not independently authorize capital punishment.

12. The Judicial Intelligence Service may exercise only the judicial powers lawfully delegated to it.

13. Unauthorized judicial record manipulation shall constitute criminal conduct where the applicable offense elements are established.

14. An altered judicial record shall remain subject to the official record-integrity and review procedures established by law.

15. Government officials and private contractors shall remain accountable for corruption and unlawful misuse of authority.

16. Nothing in these provisions shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

17. Where offenses overlap, applicable merger and double-jeopardy protections shall govern.

18. These provisions remain in force unless amended or repealed through lawful state authority.
