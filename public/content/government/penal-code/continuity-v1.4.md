# LEGISLATIVE CONTINUITY AND AUTHORITY

1. Sections 7–8 shall be interpreted consistently with Sections 1–6 of the Rosefire Penal Code.

2. Firearm regulations shall remain governed by Section 12 of the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

3. Controlled-substance regulations shall remain governed by Section 13 of the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code.

4. The official weapon classification schedule shall determine Classes I–IV.

5. The official controlled-substance schedule shall determine applicable substance classifications.

6. Lawful firearm possession shall not independently establish criminal intent.

7. Lawful cannabis possession, cultivation, transport, and sales shall remain protected within the scope authorized by law.

8. Commercial licensing violations shall be distinguished from criminal offenses where the required criminal elements differ.

9. Searches, seizures, arrests, and forfeitures shall comply with applicable civil rights and judicial procedures.

10. Criminal fines shall remain within the applicable statutory ranges.

11. Sentences exceeding six hours shall require express statutory authorization and appropriate judicial determination.

12. No fixed-term sentence shall exceed seven real-world days, equivalent to 168 hours, for a single sentencing event.

13. These offenses shall not independently authorize life imprisonment unless a separately established qualifying capital offense applies.

14. Capital punishment shall remain subject to the extraordinary eligibility and human judicial procedures established by the Civil Rights & Justice Code.

15. No provision shall authorize conduct prohibited by Rosefire's community conduct rules.

16. Where multiple offenses punish the same conduct, applicable merger and double-jeopardy protections shall govern.

17. These provisions remain enacted and in force unless amended or repealed through lawful authority.
