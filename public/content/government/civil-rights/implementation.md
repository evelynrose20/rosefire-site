# LEGISLATIVE IMPLEMENTATION AND INTERPRETATION

The following matters shall be governed by subsequent sections of the Rosefire Civil Rights & Justice Code or separate duly enacted legislation:

- Police use of force, including specific limitations governing lethal and less-lethal force.
- Criminal processing, charging procedures, and sentencing authority.
- Judicial remand, bail, parole eligibility, and sentence-reduction procedures.
- Department of Corrections institutional regulations and disciplinary procedures.
- Licensing, permit enforcement, suspension, and revocation.
- Vehicle towing, impoundment, and permanent forfeiture.
- Firearm possession, registration, classification, and lawful carry.
- Controlled substances and regulated commercial activity.
- Hunting, fishing, wildlife conservation, aviation, and maritime restrictions.
- Judicial filing deadlines, appeals, and civil proceedings.
- Judicial technology authorization, oversight, and automated decision limits.
- Official state maps, territorial jurisdictions, and restricted areas.

## Supremacy and Consistency

1. These provisions shall serve as the foundational civil-rights and justice standards within their defined scope once enacted.

2. Departmental policies, administrative procedures, and contractual agreements shall not override the protections established by this Code.

3. Where a subsequent law creates an exception to a protection established herein, that exception must be expressly authorized and interpreted according to its stated scope.

4. An invalid provision shall not automatically invalidate the remaining provisions of this Code.

5. Amendments shall be formally recorded and published through the recognized legislative process of the State of San Andreas.
