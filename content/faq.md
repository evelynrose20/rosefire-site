# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Rosefire finished?

No. Rosefire is in active development. The first outside players will be founding testers helping us discover problems that are difficult to find while developing alone.

## Do I need a full server population to play?

No. Rosefire is specifically designed to remain playable at very low population. Other players should make the world richer, not determine whether basic gameplay functions.

## Is Rosefire an MMO?

Rosefire is a FiveM roleplay server with **MMO-like persistent systems** underneath the RP. Professions, production, ownership, collections, projects, and long-term character goals are intended to give residents meaningful things to pursue between social scenes.

## Do I need to memorize all the lore?

No. Public lore should be easy to understand, and deeper information should be discoverable through play.

## Where do I report a bug?

The Discord bug-report flow will be documented here once the Discord setup is finalized.
